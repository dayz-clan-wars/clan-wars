# The kill feed — deploy runbook

No migration. The bot gains a poster (`kill-feed-tick.ts`) that turns new PvP rows in
`kills` into embeds in `#kill-feed` (Feeds category, id `1546919850583261215`): killer and
victim with the clan each was in at that instant, weapon, distance, and a running tally of
the killer's kills and the victim's deaths in the newest season. Deaths with no killer and
self-kills are never posted. Nothing in the embed is a position.

1. **Set `KILL_FEED_CHANNEL_ID`** in the host `.env`. Off by default, like the feed and the
   war log: without it the tick does nothing.

2. **Do NOT seed the `kill-feed-poster` cursor.** Its first run seeds itself at the newest
   kill's event id and posts nothing — the opposite of the stats projectors, which replay
   on purpose. `kills` holds history (58 rows from the launch-day reparse), and a poster that
   replays history announces last week to a public channel. Expect one log line:

       kill feed: cursor seeded at the head; history is not posted

   To deliberately post from an earlier point, set `consumer_cursors.last_event_id` for
   `kill-feed-poster` to the `events.id` AFTER which kills should post — with the bot
   stopped, and knowing every PvP kill after it goes out.

3. **Pull and restart the bot**: `git pull && sudo systemctl restart clan-wars-bot`. The bot
   role already has View Channel, Send Messages and Embed Links guild-wide.

4. **Verify** on the first PvP kill after the restart: an embed in `#kill-feed`, and
   `kill feed posted 1` in the journal within a tick of `kills: 1 written`. A `kill feed
   blocked at kill event N` line means the channel refused the post; nothing behind it
   posts until it succeeds.
