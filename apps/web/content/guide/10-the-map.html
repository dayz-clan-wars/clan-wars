What the server can see

Positions come from the server log once every five minutes. Every pin shows its age — "14 min ago", "3 h ago", "yesterday". "Last known" means exactly that. You cannot use this map to track a live fight, and that is on purpose: it is a strategic map, not live tracking.

Only your own clanmates' positions are ever visible to you, plus intruders inside your own watch zone. A solo declarant gets the same intruder layer for their own base. No player's position is shown to anyone outside their clan for any other reason — not on a public page, not in Discord, not in an announcement.

Pins

Press and hold to drop one. Pick an icon — loot, vehicle, enemy seen, meet here, danger, note — add text if you want, and everyone in the clan sees it with your name and the time. Anyone in the clan can delete a pin. Pins expire after 7 days. Nothing about a pin ever leaves the clan.

Things the map is honest about

  • A member who logged off an hour ago has an hour-old pin. A member gone a week has a week-old pin, dimmed. The map never implies someone is there when it does not know.
  • A raid in progress will show up five to seven minutes late at best. Use it to know who has been around your base, not to fight.
  • Dormant clans keep their map. Supplies stop; the map does not — you need it to go back and re-raise.
