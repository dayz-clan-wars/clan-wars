What the server can see

Positions come from the server log once every five minutes. Every pin shows its age — "14 min ago", "3 h ago", "yesterday". "Last known" means exactly that. You cannot use this map to track a live fight, and that is on purpose: it is a strategic map, not live tracking.

Only your own clanmates' positions are ever visible to you, plus intruders inside your own watch zone, and any player with an open bounty on them while they are online. A solo declarant gets the same intruder layer for their own base. No player's position is shown to anyone outside their clan for any other reason — not on a public page, not in Discord, not in an announcement.

The GPS grid

The grid on the map is the same grid as the GPS in your hands: three digits across from the west edge, then three digits down from the north edge at the top. The number you read off the in-game GPS is the square you find on the map, and the reverse. The readout at the bottom of the map shows the GPS reference and the metres under your pointer (the middle of the map on a phone), so you can read a spot off the map and walk to it. Tap the readout to copy it.

Search

The search box at the top of the map takes whatever you have. Pick a result and the map flies there.

  • A GPS reference: 072 087, as the GPS shows it. Type the two numbers with a space between them.
  • Metre coordinates: 7216 5706, the east and north numbers the server uses, for when someone gives you a spot in metres.
  • A place name: a town, a village, a landmark. Start typing and it fills in.
  • A bunker: bunkers are listed with the places.
  • An item: a rifle, a jacket, a can of beans. You get a card with what it is, its weight and size, and what fits it, and the map shows every spot where it can spawn, with the tiers it belongs to.
  • A building or object: pick it from the Models tab and the map shows every one of them on the server.

When you search a GPS reference or metres, the spot gets a mark, and if you can drop clan pins the mark offers Pin it to pin that spot.

The world layers

Open the layers panel and the world layers in it let you switch on what the server itself has placed: the bunkers, the vehicles that spawn on the roads, crash sites, loot buildings by type and by tier, wrecks, player spawns and the contaminated areas. Infected and Animals draw the circles the server spawns infected and animals in. None of this comes from another site. It is read from our own server's files, so it shows what this server does, including the changes we have made to vanilla. When we change the loot or the map, the layers are rebuilt from the new files.

Loot buildings are coloured by the kind of loot they hold, and only military has a colour of its own. Tap a building and its popup shows its tier and what kind of loot it takes. The Tiers chips filter what is drawn. On this server the tiers run by region: the north is Tier 1, the middle is Tier 2 and the south is Tier 3. So the Tier 3 chip shows the southern buildings and the Tier 1 chip the northern ones. Bunkers and contaminated areas show at every zoom; the rest show once you zoom in.

A layer shows where things can be. A vehicle spot is where one may appear, not a promise that one is there now, and a loot building is not a promise of loot. Wrecks and crash sites hold the loot their events give them, which is why an item's card lists the events that carry it.

The ruler

The ruler button turns the map into a tape measure. Click a start, then click as many points as you like; the bar shows the length of the whole path, in metres under a kilometre and in kilometres past it. The bar also gives the bearing of the last leg you measured. Clear ends the measurement. Nothing you measure is saved or shared.

Pins

Press and hold (right-click with a mouse) to drop one where you pressed, or use Pin here to drop one at the centre of the map — move the map and the pin moves with it until you drop it. Pick an icon — loot, vehicle, enemy seen, meet here, danger, note — add text if you want, and everyone in the clan sees it with your name and the time. Anyone in the clan can delete a pin. Pins expire after {{PIN_TTL_MS|days}}. Nothing about a pin ever leaves the clan.

Things the map is honest about

  • A member who logged off an hour ago has an hour-old pin. A member gone a week has a week-old pin, dimmed. The map never implies someone is there when it does not know.
  • A raid in progress will show up five to seven minutes late at best. Use it to know who has been around your base, not to fight.
  • Dormant clans keep their map. The crate stops; the map and your flag do not. You need both to go back and re-raise.
