Fifty marks on your record. Earned once, kept for good, and every one of them is proved from the server's own log — nothing here is claimed, only witnessed.

What they are

An achievement is a fact about what you did, in four kinds. Solo marks are about showing up and getting around. Survival marks are the map's doing: what you built and what killed you. Combat marks are kills, streaks and flags taken by hand. Clan marks belong to the clan, and every full member at the moment they are earned shares them; leave later and you keep the ones you were there for.

Friendly fire counts toward nothing, with one honest exception you will find below. Nothing here scores a point on the scoreboard. It is a record, like your stats.

Where to see them

Your profile page carries the wall: earned in gold with the date, the rest dimmed with how far along you are. Your own page shows the three you are closest to. A clan's page shows its twelve. Each unlock is announced in your clan's channel, in #achievements, and to you by DM.
