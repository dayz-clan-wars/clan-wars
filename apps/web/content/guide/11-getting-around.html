Travel points

The outhouses and well pumps spread across the map are travel points — {{TRAVEL_POINTS|n}} of them. Every one is on the map as its own layer.

  • Outhouse: stand inside.
  • Well pump: stand on top.
  • Your clan's flagpole: stand beside it. An active clan's declared pole is a travel point too — while the clan is active and its flag is up. Reserved, dormant or raided, and the door is shut until the flag flies again. Poles are not on the travel-points layer: your own is on your map as your base, and nobody else's is shown at all.

Log out, log back in

Log out while standing on the spot. When you log back in, you are at the Fast Travel Hub. Nothing happens if you just walk in and out — the move only happens on a relog.

Pick your destination

The Hub is a horseshoe of 31 porta-potties, one per town, in alphabetical order: left arm, back wall, then right arm. Stand inside the one you want, log out, log back in, and you arrive near that town.

Going somewhere else afterwards? Log out at any outhouse or well pump to come straight back to the Hub. Travel as often as you like.

Supplies at the Hub

Basic survival gear is laid out by the arrival point: bandages, vitamins, maps, hatchets, pickaxes, sledgehammers, whetstones, rope, wire, nails, locks, flags, fuel canisters, and a full set of repair kits — duct tape, epoxy, sewing, leather, tire, electronics, weapon cleaning, blowtorch.

The Hub is shared, neutral, and a no-combat zone. Other survivors can be standing in it when you arrive, but nobody may hit, kill or trap anyone there — it is an automatic {{HUB_BAN_MS|hours}} ban. See Fair play.

Fast travel and the war

  • A relog through the Hub is a normal logout as far as the rules go. The combat logging rule still applies: no travelling out of a fight.
  • Your map pin jumps with you. Clanmates will see you appear near your destination on the next five-minute fix.
  • The spot you arrive at is normal ground. If someone has declared a base within {{WATCH_ZONE_RADIUS_M|m}} of one, arriving there counts as being at their base, and their map will say so.
