Getting in

  • Join the Discord, log in with Discord, link your gamertag with three emotes in order.
  • One gamertag per Discord account, one Discord account per gamertag.
  • You cannot unlink while in a clan.

Founding

  • Three linked players each lower and raise the white flag at one unbound pole within {{CEREMONY_WINDOW_MS|minutes}}.
  • Claim on the site within {{CLAIM_WINDOW_MS|hours}}: name, tag, one of the {{FLAG_POOL_SIZE|n}} flags, roster.
  • Raise your flag at that pole within {{ACTIVATION_WINDOW_MS|hours}} of claiming, or the reservation lapses.
  • Reserved and active clans get the same supply kit at every restart, your clan flag included. Rank buys nothing.

Bases

  • Every flagpole is a base. Declared is private; everything else is public, with the flag flying there.
  • One declaration per player: your clan's base, or one solo base.
  • New poles get {{NEW_POLE_GRACE_MS|days}} before going public; released poles get {{RELEASED_POLE_GRACE_MS|n}}.
  • No two declared bases within {{MIN_BASE_SPACING_M|m}}. First declared wins.
  • A solo base becomes a clan base by ceremony, only if the declarant takes part.
  • Joining a clan releases your solo declaration.
  • A solo declaration lapses after {{SOLO_LAPSE_MS|days}} without a raise by the declarant.

Raiding

  • A raid is a non-member lowering a clan's flag at its declared base.
  • A clan scores at most once against the same victim in any {{RAID_DEDUP_MS|hours}}.
  • Solo raiders are announced but score nothing.
  • Base damage is on Friday 00:00 UTC to Monday 00:00 UTC. Boosting in and lowering flags is legal all week.
  • Announcements name clans and players, never places.

Defending

  • Flag down: re-raise within {{FLAG_DOWN_MS|hours}} for a defense, or go dormant.
  • No member raise for {{DORMANT_AFTER_MS|days}}: dormant.
  • Dormant: supplies stop, unranked, name, tag and flag kept.
  • Any member raise revives you with points intact.
  • Dormant {{DISBAND_AFTER_DORMANT_MS|days}}: disbanded, flag back to the pool; name and tag held until season end.
  • Only a member's raise counts. Getting raided never costs your name, tag or flag.

The scoreboard

  • Only raids score: 200 on #1, 100 on the bottom or an unranked clan, sliding between. Dormant clans cannot be raided.
  • Week: Monday 00:00 UTC to Monday 00:00 UTC. Top three that week are Alphas.
  • Season: wipe to wipe. Champion recorded forever; points reset; clans do not. A wipe clears every base; the first member raise of your flag after it becomes your new base.
  • Scoreboard, directory, war log, clan pages and player profiles are public. Coordinates and positions never are.
  • Kills are stats, never points.

Running a clan

  • Ten players, hard cap.
  • Joiners are pending until seen within {{JOIN_PRESENCE_RADIUS_M|m}} of the base. Invites, requests and no-shows expire after {{PENDING_EXPIRY_MS|days}}.
  • A leader removed from the Discord is replaced at once by the longest-serving officer, then member.
  • Leaving or being kicked: 3-day cooldown. Disbanding: none.
  • Leader silent {{LEADER_SILENT_MS|days}}: an officer can claim; the leader has {{SUCCESSION_WINDOW_MS|hours}} to show up in game.
  • No-confidence vote: {{VOTE_LENGTH_MS|hours}}, two-thirds of all full members, ex-leader stays as officer, {{FAILED_VOTE_COOLDOWN_MS|days}} between failed votes. Kicks and transfers frozen while a vote is open.
  • Rename once per {{RENAME_COOLDOWN_MS|days}}. Old names and tags — renamed or disbanded — are held until season end.
  • Moving your base: raise your own flag at a free pole, leader confirms within {{REBIND_CONFIRM_MS|hours}}, {{REBIND_COOLDOWN_MS|days}} between moves.
  • The vault never posts a code to Discord. Departed members' locks are flagged until rotated.

Discord

  • One text and one voice channel per clan. Guest passes are voice only, {{GUEST_PASS_MS|hours}}.
  • Nicknames are gamertags with your clan tag in front.
  • No management commands. The site is the tool.

The map

  • Positions are last known, once every {{POSITION_FIX_MS|minutes}}, and always show their age.
  • You see your clanmates, your base, intruders in your {{WATCH_ZONE_RADIUS_M|m}} watch zone, public bases, and clan pins. Nothing else.
  • Pins are clan-only and expire after {{PIN_TTL_MS|days}}.

Getting around

  • Stand at an outhouse, well pump, bus stop or your own active clan's flagpole, relog, and you are at the Hub. Pick a town door, relog again. All 209 fixed travel points are a map layer.
  • The Hub is neutral and shared. Combat logging rules still apply to a fast-travel relog.

Fair play

  • No exploits, duping, glitching, cheating, alts, stream sniping, whitewalling, code brute-forcing.
  • Raidable gates, no floating, two towers high, no loot buildings, no roads.
  • Outside the window: boost in, but no dismantling and no fence-to-gate. The log knows.
  • No takeovers of declared bases. No griefing. No combat logging.
  • Staff remove people. The system does the rest.
